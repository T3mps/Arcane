# Arcane 1.0 roadmap: definition of done and the October to January schedule

**Date:** 2026-10-05. **Status:** DRAFT for the user's review. **Target:** "AAA-capable for our needs" by the end of January 2027.

This document fixes the finish line and orders the work. Every arc spec from here on cites the row of section 2 it advances. An arc that advances none is out of scope for 1.0.

## 2. Definition of done (1.0)

"Our needs" means two games:

1. **Aphelyon:** a gacha RPG with an isometric overworld and Wakfu-style in-place turn-based combat.
2. **The next game:** a single-shard MMO space sim with the Source 2 shape.

Each row's bar is what the 1.0 audit checks. "Exists" is not enough; the bar is the measurable part.

### Already at bar (no 1.0 arc needed)

- **Editor shell.** Docking, the inspector with ownership rules, the outliner, the asset browser and pages, undo/redo, the shader and material graphs, the node framework, the Hub and the project format, and crash and hang capture with a reporter window.
- **Build and hot reload.** The game as a DLL, arcbuild (MSBuild and Ninja), hot reload, and the ABI gate.
- **RHI.** NRI over D3D12 and Vulkan, the render graph, bindless resources, the GPU scene, GPU mesh culling, GPU crash diagnostics and breadcrumbs.
- **ECS.** Astra with the visitor and remap work, and the Identity GUID model.
- **2D physics.** Manifold2D, with Box2D-v3 parity and multithreading parity proven.
- **Assets.** Cook, mesh import (cgltf + meshoptimizer) and texture cook (BC7).
- **Automation.** Headless hosts, the Servitor witness, and golden-image gates.

### To build

## 4. The vertical slice (D16)

The slice is the integration test. It forces the systems to meet in December, not in an audit in late January.

- **Scope (proposal; user to pick the game):** a ship flying between a station and an asteroid field. Docking leads to an interior with 3 animated NPCs (pathing, simple behaviour), a station UI (inventory and quest panel, gamepad-navigable), engine and weapon VFX, spatial audio, and two networked players with one shard server.
- **Gates:**
  - the D1 perf budget holds;
  - a 30-minute two-player soak has no desync and no crash;
  - a cold start reaches gameplay in under 10 s from a cooked build.

## 5. Risks and how they are handled

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **The user is the bottleneck** (spec approvals, desk checks, decision forks) | Specs are written one arc ahead. Forks are batched into one sitting per arc as multiple choice with a recommended pick. Desk checks are replaced by automated golden, witness and perf gates wherever possible. |
| **Judgement-heavy arcs** (render look, animation feel) | Every such arc ships a reference scene plus goldens. For the look, the user approves captures, not code. |
| **Integration debt** | The vertical slice starts mid-December. Two weeks of hardening are reserved in January. |
| **Agent pipeline throughput** (CLI quotas, capacity, incidents; four on 2026-10-04) | Runner hardening is done (done-marker waits, no self-review, the one-turn guard, capacity backoff with a model fallback, Sonnet runner). Spare capacity comes from the Sonnet side lanes and from cloud credits ($250, expiring 2026-11-05) for Linux and CI work. |
| **Replication** is the classic long pole | It starts in November in its own lane, with the soak test as its gate from day one. |
| **Machine contention** (one desk, GPU tests serialize) | GPU-heavy arcs stay on lane A; lanes B and C stay CPU-bound where possible. |

## 6. Open decisions for the user (batch these)

1. **Slice target:** Aphelyon (iso overworld + turn-based combat) or the space sim? The schedule above assumes the space sim; Aphelyon would swap D2 space rendering for D11 tiles earlier.
2. **Upscaler:** an FSR 3 hook only, or FSR + DLSS + XeSS behind one interface?
3. **Game UI:** build our own (recommended; it fits the editor graph and tooling stack and the post-1.0 own-UI direction) or vendor RmlUi?
4. **Audio:** our own mixer on miniaudio (recommended for 1.0) or an FMOD/Wwise bridge?
5. **Navmesh:** vendor Recast/Detour (recommended) or build our own?
6. **Perf budget numbers:** confirm the dev GPU and the 1440p/60 target in D1.
