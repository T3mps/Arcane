**Summary (3 lines):**
1. UE contradicts NONE of the seven checked rulings outright. C, H and N match UE's shape; J, L-a and L-b diverge for stated, defensible reasons (keep).
2. P is the only ruling to revisit: UE keys every screenshot reference by `Platform/RHI_FeatureLevel` (a Vulkan result is a separate approved image by construction), so a perspective golden with no Vulkan lane is an unproven RHI, not a proven one. ## Notes

- **C:** UE's tracker is delta-accumulating (`RawDelta += (dx, dy)` per axis event) rather than position-differencing, so "no delta on the press frame" is emergent (reset on press) rather than an explicit rule. Arcane's explicit `lastMouse`-on-press is the position-differencing equivalent.
- **H:** the `-TangentDir` TODO at `UnrealWidget.cpp:193` is exactly the class of screen-space sign bug the plane-unproject shape avoids; do not import the legacy per-axis rings' approach.
- **L-a/L-b:** UE's `Tweak = 6` and `Fract < 0.5` are magic numbers with no comment beyond "when the grid fades"; Arcane's 8/24 px are at least stated in screen-space terms.
- **P:** `FAutomationScreenshotMetadata` also carries `bIsStereo`, `bIsSubstrate`, `Vendor`, `AdapterName`, driver versions and scalability levels (`Runtime/Core/Public/Misc/AutomationTest.h:724-762`) — recorded for the report, not for the lookup. If Arcane ever needs a vendor split it should be a report annotation first, a key second.
