# Contributing to Arcane

Thank you for considering a contribution.

## Contributor License Agreement

Before your first pull request can be merged, you sign the Arcane
[Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) once. A bot comments on your first pull
request with a link; signing takes one click and covers every later
contribution.

**Why:** Arcane is source-available under the Starworks Source License, and its
versions convert to MIT over time. The CLA lets Starworks license your
contribution under those terms, offer commercial licenses, and keep a clear
record of who may contribute what. You keep the copyright to your
contribution; the CLA grants Starworks a license to it, and it does not
transfer ownership.

If you contribute as part of your job, make sure your employer allows it, or
ask about a corporate CLA.

## Pull requests

- Build and run the tests before opening a pull request (see `CLAUDE.md` and
  the README for the build commands).
- Keep each pull request to one change.
- New engine systems expose their settings through the settings/cvar system.

## Licensing of your contribution

By contributing you agree that your contribution is licensed to Starworks under
the CLA, and that it is distributed as part of Arcane under the Starworks
Source License (and, after each version's Change Date, the MIT License).
